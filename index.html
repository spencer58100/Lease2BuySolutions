Frequently Asked Questions

A lease option is a structured agreement that may allow a buyer to lease a property with the opportunity to purchase it later.

Yes. We may consider homes needing repairs depending on the property and situation.

Do buyers need perfect credit?

Yes. We may be able to work with properties that still have existing mortgage balances.
